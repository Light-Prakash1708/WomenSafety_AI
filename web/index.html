saahi.

A safety network that remembers the pattern, signals quietly and shows you help is coming.

SAAHI is an Android and iOS app. It tracks escalating harassment on a private timeline, sends SOS alerts with live location and delivery receipts, has a duress PIN for forced cancellations, Walk with me check-ins, an evidence vault and aftercare support.

If someone sent you a SAAHI alert link, open that exact link. It ends with ?t= and a code.
